# FLOWBills — Enterprise Pilot Brief
### Intelligent AP Automation for Canadian Oil & Gas

---

## Executive Summary
For Canadian oil and gas operators, manual AP processes create critical bottlenecks—AFE approval delays, UWI-invoice linkage errors, and NOV disputes with vendors. FLOWBills automates invoice intake, extracts and matches AFE and UWI data instantly, and enforces rule-based NOV detection before invoices reach human review. This 90-day pilot is designed for AP Managers and Finance Controllers to validate automation capabilities in a secure, isolated environment. Teams in the pilot phase will track invoice processing cycle times and the reduction of manual exception handling as the primary success indicators.

## The Problem
- AFE approval routing delays
- UWI-invoice linkage errors requiring manual reconciliation
- NOV disputes with vendors (Notice of Variance)
- Multi-vendor batch processing bottlenecks
- Audit trail gaps for joint venture compliance
- Time spent on exceptions rather than strategic work

## The Solution

Automate: FLOWBills eliminates manual data entry by automating invoice intake directly from vendors. It intelligently extracts and matches incoming invoices against corresponding AFEs and UWIs. Leveraging our powerful SmartSearch capability, AP teams can instantly locate cross-referenced invoices, AFEs, and UWIs across the entire database. Operators stop manually typing vendor details, matching line items, and hunting down missing well identifiers.

Validate: Our system implements rule-based NOV detection, catching discrepancies before they impact the bottom line. The workflow is streamlined: an invoice comes in, is auto-matched to the correct AFE/UWI, and if a variance is detected, it is immediately flagged as an exception and routed to the appropriate approver. Valid invoices pass through seamlessly, reducing manual validation time.

Audit: Compliance is built into the foundation of FLOWBills. Every action generates a full lineage trail, ensuring joint venture and regulatory compliance. All records are export-ready and designed to satisfy stringent audit requirements. Furthermore, each operator's data is isolated at the database layer using robust Row-Level Security (RLS), guaranteeing that your financial data remains completely confidential and secure.

## Technical Foundation

- Supabase backend: row-level security, real-time updates, audit logging
